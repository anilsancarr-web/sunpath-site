## 4. What the app is, and what it is not

The app computes solar geometry — position and shadow length — from published
astronomical algorithms, using the coordinate and moment you give it. It assumes
a level, unobstructed horizon. It does not know about hills, buildings, trees,
weather, cloud, atmospheric refraction beyond a standard correction, or anything
else in front of you.

**It is provided for general information and planning.**

Do not rely on it for:

- navigation, aviation, or maritime use;
- surveying, legal boundary, right-to-light or planning determinations;
- any decision where being wrong creates a risk to safety or property;
- judging sun exposure, UV levels, or burn time — it says nothing about UV, and
  is not a medical or health device.

Sunrise and sunset times, twilight, and shadow lengths are **estimates for an
ideal horizon** and will differ from what you observe. Treat the app as a good
first answer, and verify anything that matters.
