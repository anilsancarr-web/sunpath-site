# Privacy Policy — Sun Path & Shadow

**Last updated: 30 August 2026**

## The short version

**Your location never leaves your phone.** The sun's position is closed-form
geometry — given a coordinate and a moment, the answer can be computed exactly,
on the device, with no server and no data feed. That is not a privacy feature
bolted on afterwards; it is how the app works, and it is why there is nothing to
send.

There is no account, no login, no analytics, no advertising and no crash
reporting. The app makes no network requests of its own.

One thing does leave your phone, and only if you buy something: an anonymous
purchase record, so the app can tell that the purchase is valid. It contains
nothing about where you are and nothing that identifies you by name.

## What the app uses on your device

Each of these is asked for when it is first needed, with the reason on screen,
and the app keeps working if you say no.

### Location

Used to compute where the sun is from where you are standing. It is requested
only while the app is open, at balanced accuracy — the sun's apparent position
changes by well under a tenth of a degree across a city, so there is no reason
to ask for more precision.

**Your coordinates are used on the device and are never transmitted.** There is
no reverse geocoding: the app does not send your position to a mapping service
to find out the name of the place you are in, which is why the app labels your
position simply as your current location rather than naming your street or town.
That was a deliberate choice — the screen before the permission prompt tells you
nothing leaves your phone, and that sentence has to be true.

You can decline location entirely and choose a place on the map instead. The app
is fully usable that way.

### Camera

Used only to show the live view behind the sun-path overlay in the sky screen.
**No photo or video is captured, saved, or transmitted.** The camera frames are
drawn to the screen and discarded.

### Motion and compass

Used to work out which way your phone is pointing so the sun can be drawn in the
right place over the camera view. Read on the device, used immediately, never
stored and never sent.

## What is stored on your device

- Your settings — units, time format, theme.
- The places you have saved, and the place currently selected.
- Whether you have completed the introduction.

These are stored in the device's own secure storage (the iOS Keychain, or the
Android equivalent). They stay on the device, they are not readable by other
apps, and **deleting the app deletes them**. There is no backup to any server of
mine, because there is no server of mine.

## What leaves your device

Only purchases. If you never buy anything, the app sends nothing anywhere.

### Purchases

Payment itself is handled entirely by **Apple** or **Google**, depending on
where you installed the app. I never see your card, your billing address, or
your name.

To know whether a purchase is valid — and to restore it if you change phones —
the app uses **RevenueCat**, a subscription infrastructure provider. When you
make or restore a purchase, RevenueCat receives:

- an **anonymous identifier** generated on your device. The app does not attach
  your email, your name or any profile to it, and does not call any of
  RevenueCat's user-identification features.
- the **purchase receipt** issued by Apple or Google, and what it entitles you
  to.
- **technical information** that accompanies any internet request, such as your
  IP address, the country it resolves to, and your device model and operating
  system version.

RevenueCat processes this to validate the purchase and to keep it restorable.
Their own privacy policy is at
[revenuecat.com/privacy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/).

**No location data, no camera data and no sensor data is ever included.**

## What the app does not do

- **No advertising.** There is no ad SDK in the app at all.
- **No analytics.** No usage statistics are collected, by me or by anyone else.
  I do not know which screens you open or how often you open the app.
- **No crash reporting SDK.**
- **No tracking.** Nothing is shared with anyone to follow you across other
  companies' apps or websites, and nothing is used for advertising or shared
  with a data broker. This is why the app never shows the "Ask App not to Track"
  prompt: there is nothing it would apply to.
- **No profile.** Because there is no account, there is no record anywhere that
  ties this app to you as a person.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from them. It has no

## How long anything is kept

Data on your device stays until you delete it in the app or delete the app.

Purchase records held by RevenueCat and by Apple or Google are kept according to
their own policies and their own legal obligations — I cannot delete an Apple or
Google purchase record, and neither can they on request, because it is a
financial record.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under the GDPR (Europe), the
KVKK (Türkiye), the CCPA/CPRA (California) or a comparable law — to know what is
held about you, to get a copy, to correct it, to have it deleted, and to object
to processing.

In practice, almost everything this app touches is on your own device and under
your own control, and can be erased by deleting the app. For anything held in
connection with a purchase, email **anilsancarr@gmail.com** with the store
receipt or transaction identifier and I will act on it. Without something that
identifies the purchase there is genuinely no way to find a record, because
there is no account to look you up by.

If you believe your rights have been breached, you can complain to your national
data protection authority — in Türkiye, the Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu.
